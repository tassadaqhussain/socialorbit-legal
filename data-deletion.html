User Data Deletion

Effective date: November 26, 2026

Brightfield provides SocialOrbit users with a straightforward way to request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to Request Deletion

  1. Send an email to engr.tassadaq@gmail.com from the email address associated with your SocialOrbit account.
  2. Use the subject line SocialOrbit Data Deletion Request.
  3. Include your account email address and, if available, the username or account ID connected to the account.

What Will Be Deleted

After we verify the request, we will delete or de-identify the account profile and associated personal data that we are not required to retain, including account details and user-generated content that is eligible for deletion.

Processing Time

We aim to process valid requests within 30 days. Identity verification may be required. We may need additional time where legally permitted, and we will notify you if more time is required.

Information We May Retain

We may retain limited information when necessary to comply with legal obligations, prevent fraud or abuse, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, or maintain appropriate transaction and security records. Such retained information will be restricted and deleted when the applicable retention need ends.

Third-Party Integrations

A deletion request covers data held by SocialOrbit and Brightfield. If you separately authorized SocialOrbit to connect to a third-party service, you may also need to revoke the connection or request deletion directly from that provider.

Questions

For deletion or privacy questions, contact engr.tassadaq@gmail.com.
